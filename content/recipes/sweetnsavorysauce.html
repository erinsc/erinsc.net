sweet&savory sauce

ever bought some pre-made fried goodies, like fries, nuggets or schnitzels? wish you would have a better sauce to dip it in than ketchup or mayo? make this sauce. do it. not asking.

these amounts make enough sauce for 600g of croquettes (i have eaten that in a single sitting, yes), you can double or halve it any way you wanna.

ingredients

  • 30g sugar
  • 30g soy sauce
  • 60g cold water
  • 10g potato starch

instructions

  1. combine everything in a pot, make sure the starch completely dissolves.
  2. while stirring properly, heat the pot up, either to a near-boil or until you reach the desired thickness.
  3. let cool down. the thickness wont change much during this.

soy sauce type

personally, i like to mix light and dark soy sauces 1:1, one is too weak and the other too strong, but it doesnt matter that much.
